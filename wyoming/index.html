FAQ

Questions About Track Clubs in Wyoming

How many track clubs are in Wyoming?

We currently list 19 track and running clubs in Wyoming, including clubs in Casper, Cheyenne, Rawlins, Laramie. The directory covers youth programs, adult recreational clubs, and competitive teams.

How do I find a track club near me in Wyoming?

Use the search filter above to type your city or club name. You can also scroll through all 19 listings, each one has an address, phone number, and website link so you can reach out directly.

How much does it cost to join a track club?

It varies. Youth clubs in Wyoming typically charge $50 to $200 per season. Adult clubs range from free to $400 per year. Some fees include USATF membership and meet entries, others do not. Always ask for the full breakdown before signing up.

Are these clubs USATF sanctioned?

Some are, some are not. USATF-sanctioned clubs have background-checked coaches and liability insurance. Check each club's website or call them to confirm. For youth athletes who want to compete at regional and national meets, USATF sanctioning is important.
