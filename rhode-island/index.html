FAQ

Questions About Track Clubs in Rhode Island

How many track clubs are in Rhode Island?

We currently list 18 track and running clubs in Rhode Island, including clubs in Providence, North Kingstown, Exeter, Wakefield. The directory covers youth programs, adult recreational clubs, and competitive teams.

How do I find a track club near me in Rhode Island?

Use the search filter above to type your city or club name. You can also scroll through all 18 listings, each one has an address, phone number, and website link so you can reach out directly.

How much does it cost to join a track club?

It varies. Youth clubs in Rhode Island typically charge $50 to $200 per season. Adult clubs range from free to $400 per year. Some fees include USATF membership and meet entries, others do not. Always ask for the full breakdown before signing up.

Are these clubs USATF sanctioned?

Some are, some are not. USATF-sanctioned clubs have background-checked coaches and liability insurance. Check each club's website or call them to confirm. For youth athletes who want to compete at regional and national meets, USATF sanctioning is important.
