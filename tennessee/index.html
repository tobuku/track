Questions About Track Clubs in Tennessee

How many track clubs are in Tennessee?

We currently list 61 track and running clubs in Tennessee, including clubs in Nashville, Knoxville, Murfreesboro, Memphis. The directory covers youth programs, adult recreational clubs, and competitive teams.

How do I narrow down 61 clubs to find the right one?

Use the search filter at the top of the listings to type your city name. That will cut the list down fast. From there, check Google ratings and visit each club's website to compare programs, fees, and schedules.

What is the difference between a track club and a running club?

Track clubs train on a track with coaches and compete at organized meets. Running clubs are more casual, focused on group road runs and social running. Some clubs in Tennessee do both. We have a full comparison guide if you want the details.

How do I check race results for Tennessee athletes?

Athletic.net tracks results for track and cross country meets across the US. Each club listing on this page has a link to search Athletic.net for that club's athletes and recent performances.
