FAQ

Questions About Track Clubs in New York

How many track clubs are in New York?

We currently list 135 track and running clubs in New York, including clubs in New York, Rochester, Brooklyn, Bronx. The directory covers youth programs, adult recreational clubs, and competitive teams.

How do I narrow down 135 clubs to find the right one?

Use the search filter at the top of the listings to type your city name. That will cut the list down fast. From there, check Google ratings and visit each club's website to compare programs, fees, and schedules.

How much does it cost to join a track club?

It varies. Youth clubs in New York typically charge $50 to $200 per season. Adult clubs range from free to $400 per year. Some fees include USATF membership and meet entries, others do not. Always ask for the full breakdown before signing up.

Are these clubs USATF sanctioned?

Some are, some are not. USATF-sanctioned clubs have background-checked coaches and liability insurance. Check each club's website or call them to confirm. For youth athletes who want to compete at regional and national meets, USATF sanctioning is important.
