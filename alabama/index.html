Questions About Track Clubs in Alabama

How many track clubs are in Alabama?

We currently list 24 track and running clubs in Alabama, including clubs in Birmingham, Anniston, Tuscaloosa, Mobile. The directory covers youth programs, adult recreational clubs, and competitive teams.

How do I find a track club near me in Alabama?

Use the search filter above to type your city or club name. You can also scroll through all 24 listings, each one has an address, phone number, and website link so you can reach out directly.

What is the difference between a track club and a running club?

Track clubs train on a track with coaches and compete at organized meets. Running clubs are more casual, focused on group road runs and social running. Some clubs in Alabama do both. We have a full comparison guide if you want the details.

How do I check race results for Alabama athletes?
