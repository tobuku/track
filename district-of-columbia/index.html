How many track clubs are in District of Columbia?

How do I find a track club near me in District of Columbia?

What is the difference between a track club and a running club?

Track clubs typically train on a track facility and focus on sprints, hurdles, jumps, and middle distance events. Running clubs tend to focus on road running, group long runs, and race training. Many clubs in District of Columbia offer both. Check each club's website for their specific programs.

Do I need experience to join a track club in District of Columbia?

Most track clubs welcome beginners. Many District of Columbia clubs have programs for all ages and ability levels, from youth development to adult recreational to competitive athletes. Contact the club directly to ask about their requirements and fee structure.
